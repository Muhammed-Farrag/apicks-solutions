Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with A-Picks Solutions.

How quickly can you set up appointments?

We typically start outreach within 24 hours of your discovery call and book your first appointments within 48-72 hours.

What's your appointment qualification rate?

Our appointment-to-close ratio averages 35-45% depending on your specific market and criteria.

Do you work with international investors?

Yes! We serve wholesalers and investors nationwide and have experience with cross-border deals.

Can I see real results before committing?

Absolutely. We offer a performance-based trial period so you can see our results firsthand.

How are your agents trained?

Every agent goes through a structured onboarding program covering scripts, objection handling, CRM tools, and real estate terminology before working live leads.

All plans include dedicated callers, weekly or daily reporting, and CRM lead delivery. Skip tracing and acquisition management are available as add-ons.

Yes, our plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.
